# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-07-30

First public release.

- Radial dial UI on the phone: drag apps/folders/files onto it from the PC, press to launch
- Volume control by dragging the dial
- Device pairing: PC shows an Allow/Deny popup on first connection, remembers approved devices
- Paired device management in PC settings (view and unpair)
- Auto-update on the PC app
- Windows PC agent (portable exe + optional installer) and native Android phone app
